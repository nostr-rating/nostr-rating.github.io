Keeping your account safe

Nostr-Rating has no accounts or passwords managed by an operator. Your account exists only on the devices in your hands. This page explains how that works and what to do when something goes wrong.

The essentials

  1. To get started, just press "Start on this device".
  2. Write the "recovery words" (12 words) that appear on a sheet of paper and put it away. You can start without writing them down, but then you cannot restore your account if you lose all your devices.
  3. If you play on both a phone and a computer, registering the second device too gives you peace of mind.

In everyday matches, you never have to think about keys or passwords.

What it means to have no operator

In a typical service, the operator's servers manage your account. If you forget your password, the operator restores it for you. In exchange, if the operator's servers are breached, everyone's account is at risk, and if the operator quits, the service ends.

Nostr-Rating has no such operator servers. Your account is held by your devices, so no operator can restore your account or act on it without you. You are the one who protects it, and it is built to be easy to protect on your own.

Getting started

  1. Press "Start on this device".
  2. Your recovery words appear. Write them down on paper.
  3. To check that you wrote them correctly, enter 3 of the 12 words.
1harbor2velvet3orbit4candle 5meadow6pilot7garlic8tunnel 9silver10anchor11maple12rhythm

This is a sample of how they are shown. The real words are newly made for each device, different every time.

You can also start without writing down your recovery words. However, the words are not kept on the device either, so you cannot write them down later. If you lose all your devices, the account cannot be restored.

Registering a second device

  1. On the new device, choose "I already have an account". A QR code appears.
  2. On a registered device, press "Scan QR" in Settings and scan that QR code. Then press "Add this device".

This does not use your recovery words.

When the operator or the app's URL changes

Your account itself is not the URL where the app runs, but a record kept on the Nostr relays. The record lists your registered devices and a mark of your recovery words. Even if the operator goes away or the URL where the app runs changes, this record stays as it is.

On the other hand, device keys are stored on the device separately for each URL, and a page at a different URL cannot read them. So even on the same phone, a page at a new URL counts as a different device. The device list shows the same phone once for each URL.

When you are not sure you can trust the new URL

Once you register a page at a new URL, that page can play matches as you. If it turns out to be a malicious page, what it can do is the same as when a device is stolen.

  • It can: send match results as you until you revoke it (up to about one day), and revoke your other devices.
  • It cannot: read device keys stored for pages at other URLs, or take over the account without the recovery words.

If you notice, revoke it from another device or restore your account with your recovery words. Whatever URL you move to, never enter your recovery words into the usual page.

If you did not make recovery words, you cannot move your account once the old URL stops opening. If you linked X or GitHub, a new account can carry over your past seasons' records.

The URL you can trust most is one you run yourself. The app's source code is public: make a copy (fork) on GitHub and publish it with GitHub Pages, and you can run it at your own URL for free. If you leave the settings unchanged, it uses the same relays as everyone else, so you can play against people using other URLs as usual. Even if nobody else runs the app any more, the service goes on as long as you run it.

When something goes wrong

I lost or broke my phone

On another registered device, open the "Device list" and press "Revoke" for the lost device. Even if someone picks it up, they can no longer play as you on that device.

If you have no other registered device, restore your account with the steps in "I lost all my devices" below.

I lost all my devices

Open the app on a new device and choose "I already have an account". A QR code appears. On another device (such as a computer), open the recovery tool, enter the 12 words, and then scan that QR code. If you only have a phone, see "Opening the recovery tool on a phone" below. All of the account's keys are replaced, and only the new device can be used.

At this point, new recovery words are shown. The old words no longer work, so write the new ones down on a new sheet of paper.

The recovery tool is a single file (nostr-rating-recovery.html) that you download from the releases of the app's repository. Open the downloaded file in a browser to use it. Links to download it are also on the screen after "I already have an account" in the app, and in Settings.

Opening the recovery tool on a phone

On a phone, opening a downloaded file in a browser takes a few extra steps. The steps may differ by model and version.

On Android

  1. Open a file manager app (such as Files by Google).
  2. Tap the downloaded nostr-rating-recovery.html.
  3. If asked which app to open it with, choose a browser such as Chrome.

On iPhone

  1. Open the Files app and go to where you saved the file (such as "On My iPhone" or "iCloud Drive").
  2. Long-press or tap nostr-rating-recovery.html.
  3. If it does not open well, press the share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) and choose Safari or Chrome to open it.

When you use the new device and the recovery tool on the same phone, you cannot scan the QR code on the screen. Press "Copy request" in the app and paste it into the "device request" field of the recovery tool.

There is an unknown device in the list

When the app tells you "An unknown device was registered", check whether you remember registering it yourself.

  • If you do not, revoke that device.
  • If your own devices were revoked too, restore your account with your recovery words. Restoring makes every device registered by the attacker unusable.

Match results the attacker sent before you revoked the device (up to about one day) may remain.

I lost my recovery words

If you still have a device, you can keep playing. However, recovery words cannot be made again, so if you lose all your devices, the account can no longer be restored.

If you have no device left either, the account cannot be restored. If you make a new account and link the same X or GitHub account as before, you can carry over your past seasons' records. The rating for the current season cannot be carried over.

Rules for your recovery words

  • Write them on paper. Do not take photos of them or save them in phone notes or the cloud.
  • Do not show them to anyone. Do not tell them even to people who claim to be the operator or support. The operator never asks for your recovery words.
  • Enter them only into the recovery tool. It is a file you download and open, separate from the page you usually play on. This protects the restore step even if the usual page is ever taken over.
  • If you are worried, you can split them into 3 cards and keep them in different places. Any 2 of the 3 can restore the account, so losing one is not a problem. You can split them only on the screen where the words are shown when you start.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to make recovery words?

You can play without them. To start without them, choose "Start without writing them down" when you start. However, you cannot restore your account if you lose all your devices. Ratings reset every season, so if you are fine with starting over after losing your devices, not making them is a valid choice.

Can the operator restore my account for me?

No. The operator does not hold your account. That is also why your account stays safe even if the operator is breached.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

That device's key is deleted. You can restore it from another registered device or with your recovery words. Some browsers delete data if you do not open a site for a while, so we recommend adding the app to your home screen.

What is linking X or GitHub for?

It lets you take part in rated matches, and shows that accounts belong to the same person. Accounts without a linked account cannot take part in rated matches. A player's page also shows the records of other accounts linked to the same X or GitHub account. It is not a way to restore your account.

Do not delete the post you made when linking. If you delete it, the link is removed and you can no longer carry over your past seasons' records.

(For people familiar with Nostr) Can I use a signer app?

Yes. You can register a NIP-07 browser extension or a NIP-46 remote signer (bunker) as one of your device keys. Choose "Use a signer app" when you start, or when you register a second device. On Android, any signer app that can act as a NIP-46 bunker can be registered. Signer apps that split the key with FROST can be registered the same way, as long as they work over NIP-07 or NIP-46.

The key lives outside this page, so even if the page is taken over, the key itself cannot be carried off. However, this cannot prevent the page from asking for signatures while it is open. With a signer app that asks for confirmation on every signature, you can check on the spot what you are signing.

What this app asks a signer app to sign is a kind 23753 event whose content is just a 64-digit hexadecimal value. The app also signs the same value with a per-device key kept inside the app, and accepts only what has both signatures. So even if another site asks for a signature of the same form and you approve it, it cannot add a device or impersonate you. Still, do not approve requests you do not recognize.

The registered key is treated as one of this account's device keys. The npub you use in other Nostr clients does not itself become this account.
